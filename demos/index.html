Spiking Digit Jukebox

Spoken digits generated by class-conditional diffusion models on Heidelberg Digits spectrograms: a non-spiking U-Net next to one built from stochastic spiking neurons, with real recordings and the Griffin-Lim reconstruction ceiling for fair listening. Spiking Speech Jukebox

The same listening comparison carried over from digits to the Google Speech Commands vocabulary: spoken words instead of the ten Heidelberg Digits, with the non-spiking and spiking diffusion models set side by side against real recordings. Passphrase protected until the results are published.

Spiking Denoising Gallery

Nine reverse diffusion chains playing on one clock: MNIST, binarised MNIST and CIFAR-10, each denoised by a conventional U-Net, a deterministic spiking network and a stochastic spiking network. The binarised row is the interesting one, since there the readout neuron's own Bernoulli draw is the reverse step rather than an approximation of it. Passphrase protected until the results are published.
